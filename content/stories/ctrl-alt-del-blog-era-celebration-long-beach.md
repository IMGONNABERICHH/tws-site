The blog era got its flowers this weekend. On Saturday, September 12, F&M Bank Amphitheater in Long Beach hosted "CTRL + ALT + DEL: A Blog Era Celebration," a one-day festival built around the mixtape-and-message-board music movement of the late 2000s and early 2010s, back when a co-sign from the right blog could break an artist overnight.

The lineup read like a scroll through the golden age of DatPiff and music blogs: Miguel and Big Sean headlined, with Dom Kennedy and Curren$y right behind them, joined by The Cool Kids, Gym Class Heroes, Chip Tha Ripper, Pac Div, Casey Veggies, U-N-I, and Max B. Doors opened at 5:30 p.m. with music starting at 7, and the amphitheater filled with a crowd that grew up downloading mixtapes as much as buying albums.

Beyond the performances, CTRL + ALT + DEL leaned into nostalgia as a theme, celebrating how blogs, mixtapes, and online communities reshaped how fans discovered music and how artists built their careers outside of traditional label systems. It's a lineage that runs directly into today's creator-driven streaming landscape, and the festival made the throughline from that era to now impossible to miss.

For a city with deep hip-hop roots, hosting a lineup this era-defining felt right at home. CTRL + ALT + DEL made the case that the blog era never really ended, it just found a new venue.
