Goldie is back. “Jungle Daze,” featuring jazz vocalist Natalie Williams, is out now via London Records, arriving alongside an official music video and a premiere from Nemone on BBC Radio 6 Music.

Williams is no stranger to Goldie’s world — she appeared on “I Adore You” from his 2017 album The Journey Man. Here, her voice is folded into a widescreen production that moves from time-stretched breaks into something more cinematic and open-ended. It reaches back toward the 90s without living there.

Goldie has framed the record as a nod to the younger generation now discovering jungle — a reminder of where the sound came from, from someone who was there when it was built.

The single marks the opening of a new chapter, with more music promised in the coming months. The next marker is Dare To Dream, a one-off orchestral performance at London’s Royal Festival Hall on November 14 as part of the EFG London Jazz Festival. Staged across two performances, it pairs Goldie with the Elysian Collective, a choir, and special guests still to be announced, and is expected to premiere unreleased material — “Jungle Daze” included.

Three decades in, from Timeless (1995) through orchestral works like Sine Tempore, the instinct has stayed the same: expand, don’t consolidate.

“Jungle Daze” by Goldie is out now via London Records.
