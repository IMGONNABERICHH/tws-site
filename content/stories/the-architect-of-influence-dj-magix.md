DJ Magixx is a producer, songwriter, DJ, and podcast host building a platform across music and media. He hosts "The Power Play" on iHeartRadio—a podcast dedicated to exploring innovative ideas and alternative perspectives outside the mainstream. With over 30K followers, his work spans collaborations with major brands and networks including Fox, iHeartRadio, BET, ESPN, Warner Music, and others. Through his production work, podcast presence, and strategic partnerships, Magixx has positioned himself as a multifaceted creative entrepreneur connecting the dots between music, culture, and business—demonstrating how modern creators build influence across multiple channels.

Most artists pick one lane and stay there. Magixx did the opposite. He built a system where production feeds the podcast, the podcast builds his audience, and the audience makes him valuable to brands. Each piece works together. That's not talent, that's strategy. Real money in this space doesn't come from being the best DJ or the best producer. It comes from owning the relationship with your audience across multiple channels. He's not competing on one dimension. He's built leverage by stacking value. That's why the major label deals and brand partnerships keep coming. He understands the full game and operates with intention across every channel.
